# `sitemap.ts`

Next.js [`MetadataRoute.Sitemap`](https://nextjs.org/docs/app/api-reference/file-conventions/metadata/sitemap): home, regional price pages, `/nyheter`, and every article slug from `NEWS_INDEX`. News entries include **`lastModified`** from each article’s `publishedAtIso` so Google can trust freshness signals. See [docs/seo/gsc-follow-up.md](../docs/seo/gsc-follow-up.md).
